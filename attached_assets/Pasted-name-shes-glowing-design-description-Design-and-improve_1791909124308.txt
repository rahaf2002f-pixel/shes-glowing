She’s Glowing — Design Skill

Use this skill when designing, updating, or reviewing the She’s Glowing beauty e-commerce website.

● Keep the brand name exactly as She’s Glowing.

● Create an elegant, feminine, modern beauty-store identity.

● Use blush pink, ivory, cream, white, and subtle champagne-gold accents.

● Use readable, elegant typography and consistent spacing.

● Keep the design polished and cohesive across all pages.

● Use high-quality beauty/product imagery where available.

User experience and accessibility

● Make navigation simple and consistent.

● Ensure readable contrast, labels for form fields, keyboard-friendly controls, and touch-friendly buttons.

● Make the site work well on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

● Prevent horizontal scrolling, overlapping content, and clipped components.

1. Inspect the existing project, framework, components, and database connections before editing.

6. Preserve authentication, product data, cart logic, and database configuration.

7. Test key pages and interactions at mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes.

8. Fix visible layout issues, broken interactions, and errors caused by the changes.
